# Progression Research

## Verified milestones

- The official store describes 120+ Artifacts and 96+ Prestige upgrades, showing that the full companion needs a normalized database rather than UI-embedded constants. [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4078200/)
- Official v1.1.0 notes state that Ascension grants 25 PP per Ascension rank, and identify several Tier 6 upgrade effects. [Official v1.1.0 notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4078200/allnews/)
- Official v1.3.0 notes describe Endless Artifacts and revise Tier 6 World Spire upgrades. [Official v1.3.0 notes](https://steamcommunity.com/app/4078200/allnews/)
- The community Prestige index identifies Tier 2, 3, 4, and 6 unlock thresholds of 12, 45, 120, and 250 PP spent, respectively. These thresholds are **community/high confidence**, not yet direct-build verified. [Prestige index](https://dwarfeatsmountain.gamedb.wiki/prestige/)

## First-Ascension implication

The supplied fixture targets first Ascension. The first optimizer must therefore distinguish permanent PP acceleration, artifact consistency, key/Triple Seal Gate progress, and immediate damage. It must not issue a route recommendation until the relevant equation or scoring heuristic is marked and explained.

## Deferred until supported

PP/hour forecasts, reset recommendations, boss viability, exact milestone values, and route ordering require either an exact formula or explicitly experimental personal-run calibration. They are not Phase-2 claims.
